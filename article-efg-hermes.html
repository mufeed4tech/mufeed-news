EFG Hermes Reports Strongest Quarter Since 2019

NE Nourhan El-Sayed Business Desk · August 16, 2026 · 3 min read

Investment bank EFG Hermes posted its strongest quarterly results since 2019, driven by a rebound in regional equity trading volumes and a pickup in advisory fees from mergers and acquisitions activity.

Management pointed to renewed listing activity on the Egyptian Exchange as a key driver, alongside continued growth in the firm's non-bank financial services arm.

"Deal flow dried up for a while. It's clearly back, and clients are moving quickly to get ahead of it."

Executives said they expect the momentum to continue into the next quarter, citing a healthy pipeline of planned share offerings across the region.
